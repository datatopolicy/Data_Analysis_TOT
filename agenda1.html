Course Agenda

22–26 March 2027 · Kigali, Rwanda — detailed run-of-show for all five days.

Five days, from raw data to trainer-ready

Each day runs 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM with a working group analysis project threaded throughout. Sessions marked in orange are breaks.

Note: Timings below are the facilitator's detailed run-of-show. All practicals use the synthetic Demoland datasets — no real patient data is used at any point.
