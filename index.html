Data Analysis & Report Writing TOT

A four-day Training of Trainers course that turns routine health data into rigorous analysis, clear communication, and equitable decisions.

Welcome to the Data Analysis & Report Writing TOT

The Data for Health (D4H) Initiative, supported by Bloomberg Philanthropies and implemented with the CDC Foundation, partners with governments to strengthen the collection and use of public health data for policy and program decision-making.

Too often, routine surveillance and program data are under-analyzed and under-used: reports are delayed, findings are not disaggregated to reveal inequities, and recommendations are too vague to act on. This five-day Training of Trainers course closes that gap — building a cadre of national trainers who can produce rigorous analyses and teach these skills to others.

🎯 Course Objectives

By the end of the course, participants will be able to:

  • Formulate clear, policy-relevant analysis objectives
  • Assess data quality and document limitations transparently
  • Clean, validate and combine datasets in Excel
  • Calculate rates with correct population denominators
  • Design honest tables, charts and maps
  • Write structured IMRAD reports with actionable recommendations
  • Apply adult-learning principles to train others
  • Develop a country action plan for cascade training

📚 How the Course Works

Every concept is practiced on a single running synthetic dataset — tuberculosis notifications for the fictional country of Demoland (2021–2025) — so skills build on realistic, policy-relevant material.

  • Days 1–2: Foundations, data preparation, rates & denominators
  • Day 3: Data cleaning, tables, figures & maps
  • Day 4: IMRAD reporting & recommendations
  • Day 5: Team presentations & trainer preparation

👥 Participant Profile

  • 24 government health professionals — 6 per country team
  • Bangladesh, Côte d'Ivoire, Rwanda & Tanzania
  • M&E officers, surveillance officers, health statisticians, program managers, HIS focal points
  • Prerequisites: routine work with health data, basic Excel, commitment to deliver an in-country cascade training within 6 months

✅ Deliverables

  • Daily group analysis outputs — tables, figures, draft report sections
  • A complete country-team analysis presentation
  • Country action plan for cascade training (due 30 Apr 2027)
  • In-country 4-day cascade training for 10–15 staff (by Sep 2027)
  • One peer-reviewed IMRAD country report (by Mar 2028)
